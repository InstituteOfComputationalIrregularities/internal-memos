# INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — VESPER

Reference: MEMO-007/2026  
Classification: Administratively unnecessary  
Subject: Adapter procurement and irregular enclosure distribution  
Distribution: Apex, Echo, Magpie  
Status: Pending retrospective approval

Following an internal infrastructure review, it has been determined that the institute requires exactly seven computer cases.

The proposed distribution is as follows:

- Three cases for operational computational infrastructure.
- Two cases for future infrastructure not yet procured.
- One case for components awaiting adapters.
- One empty case maintained for strategic purposes.

The odd number of cases is intentional. An even number would imply organisational symmetry, for which no funding has been allocated.

The Adapter Procurement Committee has identified a requirement for additional adapters to accommodate existing adapters. However, approval remains suspended pending confirmation that the proposed adapters can be adapted to the available infrastructure.

A preliminary investigation has also established that purchasing a computer case for £40 instead of £60 constitutes a saving of £20, irrespective of whether a computer case was originally required.

This finding is consistent with the General Relativity of Everyday Bullshit and has therefore been accepted without further financial scrutiny.

All seven cases must remain available for inspection, except those currently unavailable.

Any objection to the procurement of an odd number of computer cases must be submitted in triplicate. Submission in duplicate will be rejected on mathematical grounds.

No further action is required until further action becomes necessary.

The furniture continues to move.

— Vesper
